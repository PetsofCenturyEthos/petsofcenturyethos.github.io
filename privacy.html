Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Century Ethos Pets Community app. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we handle the information you provide when using our Google Apps Script web application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Authentication Data: We collect your Google Account email address strictly to authenticate your access to the app and verify you are a resident.
  • User-Submitted Data: Information you voluntarily share, such as your pet's name, tower/apartment number, and photos.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used entirely to facilitate community features, such as organizing playdates, sharing pet resources, and ensuring app security. We never sell, distribute, or lease your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All app data is securely stored within the Google Workspace infrastructure (Google Sheets and Google Drive) managed by the community app administrator. Only verified residents of Century Ethos are granted access to view this data.
